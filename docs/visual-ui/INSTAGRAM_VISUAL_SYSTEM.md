# J. Becker Mebel — Instagram Visual System

**Status:** ZATWIERDZONE — MASTER VISUAL REFERENCE  
**Etap:** 01 — VISUAL / UI / DESIGN / ART DIRECTION  
**Medium:** Instagram  
**Scope:** Profil, feed, highlights, content hierarchy and visual presentation  
**Relationship to other media:** Instagram is a distinct medium and must not be treated as a 1:1 adaptation of the website.

---

## 1. Purpose

Instagram is a customer-acquisition and brand-building channel for J. Becker Mebel.

Its primary role is to:

- make the quality of Becker Mebel visible,
- create recognition and trust,
- present completed furniture and interiors,
- generate interest in custom furniture,
- provide social proof,
- create a path from discovery to contact,
- support the wider brand and website.

Instagram is **not** intended to function as a miniature website, catalogue, or personal-influencer account.

The commercial objective is clear:

> **Build the brand → attract attention → build trust → generate enquiries → acquire customers.**

---

## 2. Operating Reality

J. Becker Mebel is a small, craft-based furniture business. The owner is primarily a craftsman, not a content creator.

The Instagram system therefore must not depend on:

- daily content production,
- constant Stories,
- frequent Reels,
- polished workshop footage,
- staged influencer-style content,
- elaborate photography setups,
- the owner spending significant working time creating social media material.

At launch, the available asset library may be small and may consist largely of:

- smartphone photographs,
- existing photographs of completed projects,
- a limited number of new photographs,
- occasional short videos.

The visual system must therefore work **with a small real asset library** and improve naturally as more projects are documented.

The brand should never be held back waiting for a perfect content archive.

---

## 3. Content Hierarchy

The hierarchy for Instagram is intentionally different from the website.

### Primary — Real Projects

The main content is the furniture and the spaces it creates.

Priority:

1. completed interiors,
2. completed kitchens,
3. wardrobes and built-ins,
4. furniture details,
5. useful/functional details,
6. selected materials and craftsmanship,
7. occasional workshop / installation content.

The work itself is the hero.

### Secondary — Carousels

Carousels are useful when one project benefits from multiple views:

- overall room,
- second angle,
- detail,
- functionality,
- material,
- finished result.

A carousel should add information, not merely repeat the same image.

### Selective — Reels

Reels are used when movement genuinely adds value:

- opening/closing furniture,
- mechanisms,
- installation,
- transformation,
- a short walkthrough,
- a particularly interesting detail,
- occasional workshop footage.

There is **no requirement to manufacture Reels for the sake of frequency**.

### Social Proof

Opinions, recommendations, reactions, comments and other authentic proof of customer satisfaction are valuable.

They should preferably appear in context rather than becoming a repetitive series of generic testimonial graphics.

### Brand Typography / Graphic Posts

Typography is an important brand attribute, but Instagram is not the place to turn the feed into a typography catalogue.

Graphic/typographic posts are used selectively to:

- create rhythm,
- communicate a strong idea,
- introduce a project,
- reinforce the brand,
- create visual breathing room.

Photography remains dominant.

## 4. Profile Architecture

The Instagram profile is a native Instagram profile, not a website navigation system.

### Profile header

Use the platform's native profile structure:

- brand avatar,
- account name,
- Follow,
- Message,
- Contact where available/configured,
- bio,
- website/external link,
- native profile controls.

Do not invent additional branded buttons or interface elements.

### Highlights

Highlights may be used as a practical secondary navigation layer, but only when there is enough real content to justify them.

Potential categories:

- REALIZACJE
- KUCHNIE
- ZABUDOWY
- OPINIE
- PRACOWNIA
- KONTAKT

These are not mandatory. Empty or weak highlights are worse than having fewer, useful ones.

**PROCESS is not a required Instagram navigation item.**

The website can explain the full process in much greater depth.

### Pinned posts

Pinned posts are treated as a first-impression layer.

A possible launch structure:

1. Becker Mebel — who/what the brand is,
2. strongest project showcase,
3. clear path to starting a project / contact.

Exact content is to be developed later when the launch asset set exists.

---

## 5. Feed Philosophy

The feed should feel like a coherent editorial portfolio rather than a rigid repeating template.

Avoid mechanical patterns such as:

> photo → logo → quote → photo → process graphic → photo

Instead:

> project → detail → project → carousel → project → occasional typography → project → Reel → project

The grid should remain visually coherent without requiring every row to form a predetermined geometric pattern.

### Core rule

**Do not design the grid first and force content into it. Design a visual system that allows good content to lead.**

---

## 6. Photography Direction

Photography is the most important visual asset on Instagram.

The intended visual world is:

- bright,
- architectural,
- warm,
- contemporary,
- premium but believable,
- natural rather than over-stylized.

Light interiors are expected and desirable because many Becker Mebel projects use light furniture.

Wood appears as:

- a material,
- a detail,
- a contrast,
- a recognisable brand cue,

not as a requirement for every image.

Avoid turning the feed into a generic "woodworking" aesthetic with excessive:

- logs,
- sawdust,
- workshop clichés,
- rustic props,
- forest imagery,
- artificial wood textures.

Real furniture and real interiors take priority.

---

## 7. Brand Integration

The approved Becker Mebel logo system remains the master reference.

Instagram uses:

- the approved 2D logo as the standard identity,
- the approved dimensional/3D interpretation as a special visual asset,
- the approved wood/material character where appropriate.

The 3D mark may create distinctive brand moments, but it must not be stamped onto every photograph.

Branding should support the work rather than compete with it.

---

## 8. Typography on Instagram

Typography is a significant Becker Mebel brand attribute, but its role differs from the website.

On the website, typography can function as a major structural and expressive element.

On Instagram, typography is primarily used to:

- punctuate the feed,
- create editorial moments,
- frame a project,
- communicate concise statements,
- establish visual rhythm.

Text should remain short.

The image or project should normally carry the visual weight.

---

## 9. Calls to Action

Instagram should create a simple path toward enquiry.

Primary actions:

- visit profile,
- follow,
- send a message,
- use Contact where configured,
- visit the website.

The profile should not be overloaded with competing CTAs.

The website remains the deeper information environment for:

- full offer,
- detailed services,
- project information,
- broader portfolio,
- contact workflow.

Instagram creates interest and trust; the website can provide depth and conversion support.

---

## 10. What Instagram Is Not

Do not turn the Becker Mebel Instagram into:

- a copy of the website,
- a full service menu,
- a daily diary,
- an influencer account,
- a workshop vlog,
- a wall of inspirational quotes,
- a grid puzzle,
- a collection of generic Canva templates,
- a feed dominated by logos and branded graphics.

---

## 11. Launch Principle

The account should launch with the best real material available, even if the initial library is small.

Placeholder/concept imagery may be used during design exploration, but final public communication should distinguish real Becker Mebel work from conceptual imagery.

The system must allow real photographs to replace placeholders without redesigning the entire profile.

---

## 12. Approved Visual Direction

### APPROVED

- Instagram-native profile structure
- photography-led feed
- bright/editorial interior world
- real projects as primary content
- occasional carousels
- selective Reels
- authentic social proof
- selective typography
- subtle use of approved 3D logo
- restrained brand graphics
- highlights only where useful
- pinned posts as first-impression content
- website as deeper information/conversion layer
- separate Instagram design logic from the website

### REJECTED / REMOVED

- "PROCESS" as a required Highlight/navigation item
- invented branded circular buttons
- treating Highlights as website navigation
- copying the website information architecture
- forcing a rigid grid pattern
- making Reels obligatory
- making the craftsman behave like an influencer
- overloading the feed with typography or logo graphics

---

## 13. Design Principle

> **The work is the content. The brand makes the work recognisable. Instagram makes people want to know more.**

This principle governs future Instagram design decisions within Stage 01.
